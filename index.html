RXLedger Privacy Policy

Shaolo LLC · Updated 2026-11-06

shawn@shaolo.com

Who we are

Shaolo LLC develops RXLedger, including RXLedger Dev. This policy explains how the app handles information when you organize medications, use optional integrations, visit this policy page, or contact us. Contact Shawn at shawn@shaolo.com with privacy questions or requests.

Records on your device

RXLedger works without a RXLedger account. Information you choose to enter or import can include people’s names and profile details; medications, prescriptions, doses, schedules, stock and refill history; doctors and pharmacies; insurance details; notes; and photos of pills, prescription labels, cards, or people.

The app uses this information to provide the features you select. Its records and saved images are encrypted locally. Device security, app settings, and operating-system features also affect access. Reminder text, widgets, Live Activities, Siri responses, calendar events, exports, and information you share may reveal medication details outside the encrypted record store. Use the app’s name-privacy controls and your device settings to choose what is visible.

Shaolo LLC does not receive a copy of your medication database simply because you install or use the app. We do not sell your personal or health information, use it for advertising, or include advertising or behavioral-tracking SDKs.

Camera, photos, and label reading

Camera and photo access are used when you choose to capture or select an image. Prescription-label text recognition and barcode reading run on your device. RXLedger does not upload those images to a remote OCR or AI service. Images you save become part of your local records and can be included in sync, backups, or sharing you enable. Online medication searches and reference-image downloads are separate network features described below.

Apple Health and health records

On supported Apple devices, you can permit RXLedger to read medications, medication-dose records, and prescriptions from clinical health records. It reads the categories you authorize to import and organize that information. It does not write changes to your Apple Health medication records.

We do not use Health information for advertising, marketing, data brokerage, or research. Imported information becomes part of your RXLedger records. Your choices to sync, export, put information in calendars, use Siri, or search online can disclose selected information through those features. You can revoke Health permissions in Apple’s Health or Settings app. Revoking access stops future reads; it does not automatically delete records you already imported into RXLedger.

Online searches and external services

Medication and reference-image lookups send relevant search terms or drug identifiers to public reference services, including the U.S. National Library of Medicine’s RxNav and DailyMed services. Doctor and pharmacy searches can contact NLM and CMS registries, search engines, practice or pharmacy websites, and logo or image sources. These services receive the request and ordinary connection information, such as your IP address; search terms may reveal a medication or health-related interest.

Nearby-place features use a location or area you supply. With your permission, the app can obtain your current location when you request a nearby search. On iOS it uses Apple’s mapping services; other platforms can use OpenStreetMap-based services. The app does not continuously track your location, and search coordinates are not added to its medication database, sync payloads, or backups. A pharmacy you choose to save can include that pharmacy’s location.

If you configure prescription-price comparison, selected drug, quantity, pharmacy, and location details are sent to your configured pricing backend and its enabled providers, such as GoodRx or Cost Plus Drugs. Pricing requests and results are transient in the app and are not added to its sync or backup records. A backend operator or provider may keep its own logs. Websites, maps, refill portals, and phone or email apps you open receive information needed for the action you select and operate under their own privacy policies.

Optional sync, backups, and calendars

Server sync is optional. If you configure it, records and images are encrypted with your sync passphrase before being sent to the server you choose. The server receives encrypted data plus connection and storage metadata, such as IP addresses, request times, authentication information, and opaque record or image identifiers. The server operator controls its access logs, retention, and backups. Shaolo LLC does not operate that server merely by providing the app.

Where available and enabled by you, iCloud sync stores encrypted snapshots in your Apple Account’s private iCloud storage. The app can also keep recovery copies of its encryption key in iCloud Keychain. Your operating system may back up app data according to its backup settings. Apple’s services and your account settings govern those copies. Encryption does not remove the need to control access to your Apple Account, device, and backups.

If you enable a calendar integration, selected medication schedules and related text are written to the calendar you choose. That calendar may sync with Apple, Google, Microsoft, or another calendar provider. If you publish a subscription feed through your sync server, the feed contains readable calendar information: anyone with its secret URL can read it. Turning off the feed requests its removal from the connected server, but cannot recall copies already downloaded by calendar clients.

Backups exported through the app are protected by the backup passphrase you choose. Calendar files and other information you deliberately share may be readable by their recipients. You control where exported files are saved and with whom they are shared.

Updates, diagnostics, and support

The app can check GitHub-hosted update information and download compatible updates. GitHub receives ordinary network request information. RXLedger does not send your medication records with update checks and disables the updater SDK’s vendor statistics endpoints.

When you use TestFlight, Apple processes installation, usage, crash, and feedback information under its TestFlight terms and privacy policy, and can make diagnostics and feedback available to us. If you send screenshots, logs, email, or other support material, we receive what you send. Use sample or redacted records in feedback whenever possible. We use support correspondence to respond, troubleshoot, and maintain the app, and retain it as needed for those purposes and applicable obligations. Contact us to request deletion of support material we hold.

The public policy page is hosted by GitHub Pages, which can process connection information for hosting and security. The page itself contains no advertising, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit or delete records in the app, revoke device permissions, disconnect calendar services, disable sync, and export a passphrase-protected backup. Deletion hides an item from active records and, when sync runs, sends deletion markers to connected copies. Deleted content or older snapshots may remain in encrypted storage, other devices, exported files, server backups, or third-party calendars; deleting an item is not a promise of immediate forensic erasure.

Turning off sync stops future syncing and does not automatically erase existing remote copies. To remove server data and logs, contact your server operator or administer that server. To remove iCloud copies, device backups, calendar copies, or exports, use the relevant provider’s or device’s controls. Removing the app or clearing its data does not necessarily remove keychain entries, cloud backups, or copies already shared elsewhere.

You may contact shawn@shaolo.com to ask about access, correction, or deletion of information Shaolo LLC holds about you. We cannot directly access or erase records stored only on your device or a server you control. Do not email medical records to us merely to make a privacy request.

Children and medical decisions

RXLedger is intended for adults, including adults organizing medication information for people in their care. It is not intended for independent use by children under 13. If a child has sent personal information directly to us, contact us so we can address it.

RXLedger is an organizer, not medical advice. Review imported or recognized information and follow your prescriber’s and pharmacist’s instructions before making medication decisions.

Policy changes

We will update this policy when the app’s data handling changes and show the updated date. The app includes an offline copy; the public page may contain a newer version. Material changes that need additional permission will require that permission through the relevant app or operating-system controls.
